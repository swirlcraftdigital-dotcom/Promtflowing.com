Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 18, 2026

At PromptFlow, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our marketplace.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you interact with the PromptFlow marketplace:

  • Account & Purchase Data: When you purchase a premium prompt, we collect your email address to deliver your digital product, send receipts, and securely provision access to your Vault.
  • Communications: If you contact us directly via support@promptflowing.com, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: If you opt-in to our newsletter, we collect your email address to send you free weekly prompts and AI insights.

2. Automatically Collected Information & Analytics

To optimize our marketplace and provide a seamless browsing experience, we use Google Analytics (via gtag.js) and similar tracking technologies to collect metadata about how you interact with our site. This includes:

  • Browser type, device type, and operating system.
  • Pages viewed, time spent on pages, and click interactions (e.g., searches, category clicks).
  • General location data (at the city or regional level) to optimize content delivery.

This information is anonymized and aggregated. We use it strictly to improve our UI/UX, measure the performance of our prompt catalog, and refine our search algorithm.

3. Payment Processing Security

PromptFlow prioritizes your financial security. We do not store, process, or transmit your credit card details or payment information on our servers.

All transactions are securely processed directly through industry-leading payment gateways, including Stripe and PayPal. These processors adhere to strict PCI-DSS compliance standards. The only data we receive from Stripe/PayPal is a cryptographic token confirming your successful payment, alongside the email address necessary to fulfill your order.

4. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used in the following ways:

  • To deliver digital products (Prompts, Workflows) directly to your Vault.
  • To send administrative emails, including purchase receipts, security alerts, and support responses.
  • To personalize your experience and display the most relevant AI prompts.
  • To prevent fraudulent transactions and monitor against unauthorized access.

5. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location (e.g., GDPR in Europe, CCPA in California), you have specific rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request that we delete your data (Note: Deleting your data will result in the permanent loss of access to your purchased Prompts within the Vault).
  • Right to Opt-Out: You may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link at the bottom of our emails.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our privacy team at support@promptflowing.com.

Questions regarding this policy?

If you have any questions or concerns about how we handle your data, please don't hesitate to reach out to us at support@promptflowing.com.
